# Better Armor Training (OpenMW)

Get accustomed to armor by moving around in it. It's heavy, after all.

While there is a similar [Armor Training (OpenMW)](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59394) mod, I figured I could make a more performant version. So here I am, presenting you Armor Training, but *Better*.

I won't be the first to admit that armor leveling is slow in this game, neither I will be the last. But hear me out: armor isn't a shirt - it's heavy as hell. And running around in it should have some effect on your physique.

## Features

Walking, running, jumpung and swimming now will give you Training Points in the background. After reaching a Training Points milestone, you will receive a batch of experience, distributed between your armor skills, based on what gear you're wearing.

There are also caps to this way of leveling, attempting to make the mod less of a cheese factory and closer to a flavor thing with a bit of functionality. At the end of the day, wearing weighs in the shape of armor will only get you so far in combat.

## Compatibility

I would be genuinely surprised if you could find a compatibility issue with this mod.

Safe to install, update or uninstall the mod mid-playthrough.
